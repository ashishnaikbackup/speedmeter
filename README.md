## 🚀 How It Works

1. Open the site — your connection details load automatically.
2. Tap **Start Speed Test** to measure download speed, upload speed, and ping.
3. Or tap **Start Monitoring** to track your speed continuously over time.
4. Results are saved to a **recent speed history** chart for quick comparison.
