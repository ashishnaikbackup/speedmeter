What Is a Good Internet Speed?

The internet speed you need depends on how you use the internet. Browsing, video calls, streaming, online gaming and large downloads can require different amounts of bandwidth.

How Internet Speed Is Measured

Internet speed is commonly measured in megabits per second (Mbps). Download speed describes how quickly data reaches your device, while upload speed describes how quickly your device sends data. Ping measures response time and is normally shown in milliseconds.
